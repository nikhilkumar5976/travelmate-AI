{% if selected_dest %}{{ selected_dest.title }}{% elif target_dest %}{{ target_dest }}{% else %}Jaipur, Rajasthan{% endif %} (5 Days)

Archetype: Cultural & Heritage • Budget: Comfort / Moderate (~₹37,500)

⚡ TravelMate AI Ready
DAY 01 Royal Bastions & Heritage Flavors
  • 08:30 AM Artisanal breakfast with spicy Pyaaz Kachori and saffron masala chai at the iconic Rawat Mishthan Bhandar.
  • 10:30 AM Ascend to Amber Fort, marveling at the mirror mosaics of Sheesh Mahal and grand Rajput ramparts.
  • 02:00 PM Royal heritage Rajasthani Thali lunch at 1135 AD with panoramic desert fort vistas.
  • 06:30 PM Sunset golden hour photography at Jal Mahal water palace and evening gem shopping at Johari Bazaar.
DAY 02 Pink City Iconography & Astronomy
  • 09:00 AM Iconic honeycomb facade photography at Hawa Mahal followed by morning espresso at Wind View Cafe.
  • 11:30 AM Guided exploration of City Palace royal museum & UNESCO-listed Jantar Mantar astronomical observatory.
  • 02:30 PM Traditional Rajasthani feast at Handi Restaurant on MI Road trying authentic Laal Maas and Bajra roti.
  • 06:00 PM Golden sunset vista overlooking the entire Pink City skyline from the mountain ramparts of Nahargarh Fort.
DAY 03 Stepwells, Cannons & Artisan Villages
  • 08:30 AM Early morning photography at the symmetrical geometric stepwell of Panna Meena Ka Kund.
  • 11:00 AM Jaigarh Fort visit to marvel at Jaivana, the world's largest cannon on wheels, and secret underground passages.
  • 02:00 PM Traditional wooden block-printing workshop & blue pottery shopping in Sanganer craft village.
  • 07:00 PM Immersive cultural folk dance, puppet show, and royal village dining feast under desert tents at Chokhi Dhani.
DAY 04 Palace Gardens & High-Tea Culture
  • 09:00 AM Peaceful stroll through lush terraced fountains and painted pavilions of Sisodia Rani Ka Bagh.
  • 11:30 AM Explore Albert Hall Museum, admiring centuries-old Rajput armor, miniatures, and Persian carpets.
  • 02:30 PM Modern fusion brunch and chai degustation at Tapri Central overlooking Central Park.
  • 06:30 PM Evening sound-and-light spectacle at Amber Fort under the starry Aravalli night sky.
DAY 05 Spiritual Sanctuaries & Sunset Bazaars
  • 08:30 AM Serene morning prayers and white marble carvings at Birla Mandir (Laxmi Narayan Temple).
  • 11:00 AM Adventure hike through the mountain gorge to sacred Galta Ji (Monkey Temple) natural freshwater springs.
  • 02:00 PM Rooftop leisure lunch at Baradari Restaurant inside City Palace grounds.
  • 05:30 PM Souvenir treasure hunt in Bapu Bazaar for handcrafted Mojaris, camel leather goods, and bandhani textiles.
